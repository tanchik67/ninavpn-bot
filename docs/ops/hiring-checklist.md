# Growth ops — hiring triggers

Hire / outsource when MRR clearly covers the cost (rule of thumb):

| Role | Trigger | First month focus |
|------|---------|-------------------|
| Support | >40 tickets/week or SLA >24h | Macros, FAQ, chat coverage |
| Growth | Stable P0/P1 product + EN checkout live | Content calendar, affiliates outreach |
| Part-time SRE | Repeated SEV1/2 or >5 nodes | Monitoring, failovers, backups |

Until then: founder-owned support + `docs/ops/incident-playbook.md` + `scripts/health-monitor.sh`.
